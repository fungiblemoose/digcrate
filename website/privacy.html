DigCrate Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

DigCrate is made by Backspin Labs, LLC. This policy describes the current 1.x Mac App Store release and this product website.

Your library

Audio files are analyzed on your Mac. Library analysis, sets and shortlists are stored in a local database. Audio is read from the folders you choose; the planning routes described below use prompts and track metadata, rather than uploading your audio files.

Set planning

Apple’s on-device planning runs locally when Apple Intelligence is available. If you configure a model server, DigCrate sends the planning prompt and selected track metadata to that endpoint. A server on another machine receives that information over the network. Its operator and security settings determine how it is handled, so choose a server you trust.

When a language model is unavailable, rules-based planning can run locally. The current 1.x release does not include Apple Cloud planning. This policy does not describe unreleased development builds.

Usage and accounts

DigCrate does not require a Backspin Labs account. Apple handles distribution, purchases and any diagnostics available under your Apple settings. Backspin Labs does not receive your music library through a hosted DigCrate planning service.

This website and email updates

Cloudflare hosts this website and handles ordinary request information. We have not added an analytics script to this site. If you subscribe to updates, we store your email address, signup source, campaign and referrer information, request user-agent and submission timestamp through our shared Backspin Labs signup service. We use these for updates, attribution and abuse prevention. To unsubscribe, email hi@backspinlabs.com. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send.

Discovery links

The Discover feature opens search pages on Beatport, Spotify, or SoundCloud in your web browser. The app itself sends nothing to these services; once in your browser, their privacy policies apply.

Purchases

DigCrate Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the Mac App Store. We never see your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
